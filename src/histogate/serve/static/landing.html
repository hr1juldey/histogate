How the journey works

Four steps, in the order a pathologist's workflow actually goes. Nothing classifies before it's checked — that ordering is the product.

Drop a patchAny H&E square tile — or pick one of the curated examples, each chosen to show a different behaviour.
Quality gate firstIs the tile even usable? Blur, pen marks, folds and air bubbles get flagged before diagnosis — a bad input must never produce a confident answer.
Diagnosis, calibratedNine tissue classes with temperature-calibrated probabilities — when it says ninety-seven percent, it is ninety-seven percent.
See where it lookedA Grad-CAM heatmap shows the regions that drove the call — gland architecture, not the ink smear. Trust is earned visibly.
